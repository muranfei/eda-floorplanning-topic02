02 / CONCEPTS概念

BUILD THE LEGALITY GATE搭建合法性关卡

Four ideas do most of the work. 掌握下面四个核心概念，就能判断绝大部分固定轮廓问题。

01

FIXED OUTLINE固定轮廓

The chip boundary is the suitcase. It cannot stretch to fit a module. 芯片边界就像登机箱，它不会为了容纳模块而变大。

0 ≤ x and x + w ≤ W
02

POSITIVE-AREA OVERLAP正面积内部重叠

If two interiors share positive area, the layout is illegal. 如果两个模块的内部共享正面积，这个布局就是非法的。

max(0, min(r₁,r₂) − max(l₁,l₂))
03

EDGE CONTACT IS LEGAL边缘接触是合法的

Touching at a boundary has zero contact area. Touching is not overlapping. 仅在边界接触时，接触面积为零。接触不等于重叠。

C.right == E.left → legal
04
✓

LEGALITY BEFORE METRICS先合法，后指标

Density and deadspace are meaningless until containment and overlap both pass. 只有包含性和不重叠都通过，密度和空白率才有意义。
